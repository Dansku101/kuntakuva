## Findings

### 1. P1: Historical Financial Ratios Mix Municipal Boundaries

Locations: `src/lib/comparison.ts:59`, `src/app/municipality-finance.tsx:53`, `src/app/page.tsx:111` (financial section population prop).

The numerator is the reporting municipality's accounts for that year. The denominator is a population observation restated to the 2026 municipal classification. Matching observation years does not make these geographical scopes equal.

Reproduced in the browser with **Mäntyharju, 2024**, in both Tilastot and Vertailu:

- Population used by the application: **7,057**.
- Financial totals shown: approximately **EUR 20.74 million income** and **EUR 19.75 million expenses**.
- Per-resident figures shown: **EUR 2,939 income** and **EUR 2,799 expenses**.
- Pertunmaa merged into Mäntyharju on **1 January 2025**. The 2024 accounts are for pre-merger Mäntyharju; the application's population includes the subsequently merged area.
- A published 2024 statement version reports a population of **5,534**, demonstrating the materially different scope. Small revisions between preliminary and final population are a separate issue; they do not explain a difference of roughly 1,500 residents.

This understates the pre-merger per-resident figures. A general note about mergers does not make the calculation valid. This boundary issue was already a documented limitation in the previous audit; the new comparison now exposes it more directly.

**Recommended fix:** Track population and accounts geography explicitly. Use a verified reporting-year population with the same boundaries, or suppress derived per-resident figures for affected municipality/year combinations. Do not automatically sum predecessor accounts without reconciling their scope. Also review the financial trend across a merger and mark a break when observations are not like-for-like.

**Acceptance:** A Mäntyharju 2024 regression test must not divide its standalone accounts by 7,057. Unaffected municipalities must retain existing reconciled figures. Geography notes must accurately describe the denominator actually used.

Sources: [Official merger notice](https://pertunmaa.fi/paatoksenteko/paatoksenteko/mantyharjun-ja-pertunmaan-kuntaliitosselvitys/), [published 2024 statement, printed page 10 / PDF page 13](https://mantyharju.cloudnc.fi/download/noname/%7B128f9fbe-3cef-46e7-95bb-3a600ca7f8de%7D/28086), [Statistics Finland population classification explanation](https://stat.fi/fi/tilastot/tietoa-teemoittain/vakiluku).

### 2. P2: Language Distribution Omits Sami Speakers

Locations: `src/app/municipality-overview.tsx:24`, `src/lib/statfin.ts:20`.

Kielijakauma shows Finnish, Swedish, and other languages explicitly excluding Finnish, Swedish **and Sami**, but contains no Sami row.

Reproduced in **Utsjoki, 2025**: displayed shares are **53.7%, 0.6%, and 4.5%**, totaling only **58.8%**. A live query of official table 11ra returned population **1,112**, Finnish **597**, Swedish **7**, Sami **458**, and other languages **50**. The missing group is approximately **41.2%** of residents, not a rounding discrepancy.

**Recommended fix:** Request `vaesto_kieli_se` and the population denominator from the same table, municipality, year and classification. Calculate and display the Sami share; preserve nulls. Do not invent a `vaesto_kieli_se_p` field: the live metadata exposes a count, not that percentage metric. The current demographic parser assumes percentage-like values are at most 100, so counts need appropriate validation.

**Acceptance:** A Utsjoki fixture must represent all four groups and reconcile their underlying counts. Missing counts must not appear as zero. Account for rounding when checking displayed percentages.

Source: [Statistics Finland 11ra](https://pxdata.stat.fi/PxWeb/pxweb/fi/StatFin/vaerak/11ra.px/). Verified through its public API metadata and a five-metric observation query during this audit.

### 3. P2: Patch Dependencies With Known Security Advisories

Locations: `package.json:17`, `package-lock.json:6044`, `package-lock.json:6206`.

`npm audit --omit=dev --json` reported **three high-severity production dependency entries**:

| Installed Package | Reported Patched Range / Version |
| --- | --- |
| Next.js 16.3.6 | The listed Next advisories exclude 16.3.8 and later; audit proposes 16.4.0 |
| sharp 0.35.4 | 0.35.5 |
| source-map-js 1.2.1 | 1.2.2 |

These counts are package-level advisory matches, **not three demonstrated exploits in this application**. The Next image-optimization SSRF advisory explicitly excludes applications without `images.remotePatterns`; this application's configuration has no such setting. The sharp advisory concerns SVG decoding under particular Linux runtime conditions, and no untrusted SVG-processing flow was found here. Source-map-js exploitability requires attacker-supplied source maps; no such input was found. Other Next advisories include development-server origin validation and cache behavior.

**Recommended fix:** Apply a deliberate compatible dependency update, keep Next and eslint-config-next aligned, inspect the lockfile changes, and rerun tests/build/browser checks. Do not blindly run `npm audit fix --force`. Recheck advisories before choosing the exact update, and establish a deployment dependency gate.

Sources: [Next image optimization advisory](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-cjq9-62q9-8jv4), [Next development-server advisory](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-39w2-rjm5-chcv), [sharp advisory](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-wq5f-xc86-pv6w), [source-map-js advisory](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-68fv-2mgg-jv7q).

### 4. P2: A Slow Source Delays Every Comparison Section

Locations: `src/lib/comparison.ts:87`, `src/app/municipality-comparison.tsx:8`.

The comparison awaits one `Promise.all` containing every section, and the entire result sits behind one Suspense boundary. Although failures are caught per section, healthy sections cannot render until the slowest section has completed or timed out. Finance alone may perform directory, catalog, document and population requests in sequence.

Confirmed with a read-only in-memory probe of the actual comparison module: all non-finance providers resolved while finance was held pending, but no comparison result became available until finance was released. This verifies the blocking structure; it is not a production latency benchmark.

**Recommended fix:** Stream independently loaded sections with stable per-section placeholders and explicit failure/retry states. Reuse source requests where appropriate. A failed or slow Treasury request must not delay population, education or heating.

**Acceptance:** A delayed-finance integration test must show population before finance settles. A finance failure must leave other sections visible and allow a targeted retry.

## Usability And Product Improvements

These are recommendations, not additional correctness findings.

1. **Add topic navigation inside Tilastot.** Residents, finance, heating, and environment now create a long page. Use compact anchor navigation under the existing tabs, with correctly offset headings. Do not add more top-level tabs or return the detail to the overview.
2. **Make mobile headers more compact.** Search, year, title, year badge, and tabs occupy much of a small screen before the first metric. Preserve search and clear context but reduce repeated year presentation and excess vertical spacing.
3. **Support Finnish and Swedish search aliases.** Typo-tolerant Finnish search works, but `Korsholm` returned no results for Mustasaari. Use official code-keyed alternate names for both municipality pickers; add matching and keyboard tests. Do not guess aliases from similar text.
4. **Add a useful difference column to comparison.** Use absolute differences for counts/euros and percentage-point differences for shares. Avoid a generic green/red winner designation. Keep row year and unit clear. Consider total/per-resident modes for compatible datasets only after geography is fixed.
5. **Unify period and source labels.** Keep observation year, selected cutoff, and source update date distinct, and make them consistent across sections. Do not imply all figures describe the selected year.
6. **Connect map list selection to the map.** The current list changes the detail text but does not center/highlight the corresponding marker. Preserve keyboard access while linking those two views of the same place.

## Limits And Test Gaps

- This was not a full reconciliation of every municipality/year or a penetration test. Security advisories were assessed for obvious applicability, not exploited.
- Most automated coverage is data-helper, API-handler and server-render tests. Browser workflows are currently manually verified, not protected by a committed end-to-end test suite.
- No automated regression currently checks merger-aware denominators, a complete Sami-language distribution, or independent section streaming.
- Production load, assistive-technology use, multi-instance caches, all source-outage combinations and public deployment configuration were not exhaustively tested.
- Generalized map boundaries, capped/current OSM data, geography differences between sources, limited 2024–2025 financial coverage, and one-year heating coverage remain disclosed constraints. They should not be hidden by additional UI polish.

## Proposed Next Steps

### Phase 1: Trust And Reliability

Fix financial geography and the missing language category; apply dependency patches; stream comparison sections independently. Add regression cases for each change plus browser tests for search, year/view preservation, swapping/removing peers, unavailable data, and mobile overflow. This is the recommended next implementation batch.

### Phase 2: Organization And Comparison Polish

Add Tilastot topic navigation, compact the mobile header, add official Swedish aliases and meaningful comparison differences. Keep the current five top-level destinations and restrained visual style. Avoid another broad redesign.

### Phase 3: Selective New Content

After the above is stable, prioritize **age-structure trends** using the existing population source, followed by a small local saved-municipality list. Add additional energy production or financial years only after verifying their data definitions and coverage. Defer rankings, composite scores and news feeds.
